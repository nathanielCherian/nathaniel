Empyrean

Empyrean is an interactive night sky simulator and 3D star map. It shows the stars, planets, Sun, Moon and constellations as they appear in the sky from any place on Earth at any time from 3000 BC to 3000 AD, next to a 3D map of where each of them really is in space around the Sun.

      Empyrean

      The sky from any place on Earth, at any moment from 3000 BC to 3000 AD, beside a map of where each of those lights really is.

      The left view is what you would see looking up. The right view puts the same stars and planets at their true directions and distances around the Sun. Click anything to find it in both.
