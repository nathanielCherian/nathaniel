Lunation

Lunation is an interactive 3D Moon phase simulator. The left view shows the Sun, Earth and Moon in space; the right view shows the Moon as it appears in the sky from your location, with its current phase, illumination, altitude, azimuth and distance. Change the date, time and place to see any phase of the lunar month.

    About

    A live model of the Sun, Earth and Moon. The left view looks at them from space; the right view is the sky from wherever you stand on the globe. Move through time, pick any date, click the globe or search a city to move, and use Share to copy a link to exactly what you are looking at.

    Accuracy

    Positions come from compact textbook formulas (Meeus). Eclipse times are good to about a minute. Stars are approximate.
